About SANÃ

Where every view tells a unique story, the SANA Tower project stands tall in one of the most prestigious locations overlooking the waterfront, spreading over an area of 46,000 square meters and combining breathtaking panoramic sea views with elegant architectural designs. The project features three iconic towers that bring together luxury residential units, commercial spaces, and a high-end hotel tower, creating a complete destination that blends comfort, investment opportunities, and quality of life. Thanks to its extended waterfront facade, carefully designed spaces, and unique coastal location, SANA Tower goes beyond the concept of traditional real estate development; it’s an exceptional destination inspired by the beauty of the sea, offering a lifestyle full of elegance, tranquility, and lasting value.
